	<div class="inner">
		<p>Formed in January 2024, <strong>Classics, Ecocritical Research, and Environmental Scholarship (CERES)</strong> is an association aimed at bringing together scholars in classical studies and related fields who are interested in exploring what modern environmental approaches can contribute to the study of the ancient Mediterranean.</p>
	</div>
</section>

				<header class="major">
					<h3>Goals & Objectives</h3>
				</header>
				<p>CERES was created with the following goals in mind:
				<ul>
					<li>Create an online space for scholars to discuss the intersections of environmental humanities and classics</li>
					<li>Integrate more environmental and ecocritical theory to the field of classics</li>
					<li>Bridge the gap between scholarly communities in classics and environmental humanities</li>
					<li>Develop theory and method for approaching environmental questions (broadly defined) in classics</li>
				</ul>
				To achieve these goals, we hold a monthly reading and discussion group to explore approaches used in the environmental humanities. For more information, visit <a href="https://ceresecoclassics.github.io/reading_group.html">the reading group page</a>.</p>
