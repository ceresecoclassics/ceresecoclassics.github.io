<p>Dear all,</p>

<p>Thank you for your interest and participation in CERES (Classics, Ecocritical Research, & Environmental Scholarship). You can find more information about our organization at ceresecoclassics.gihub.io and on the Eco Classical Caucus digest.</p>

<p>We are excited to announce the fall themes and meetings for our reading group. Meetings will occur on the second Saturday of each month at 3:00 pm Eastern / 12:00 pm Pacific. A Zoom link is included at the bottom of this email and will be shared again in advance of each meeting. Please make a note of the following dates in your calendar:
<ul>
    <li>Saturday, September 12: Air, Wind, and Clouds</li>
    <li>Saturday, October 10: Time and Seasons</li>
    <li>Saturday, November 14: Environmental Justice</li>
    <li>Saturday, December 12: Waste Studies</li>
</ul>
At our meeting on Saturday, September 12 (3:00 pm Eastern / 12:00 pm Pacific), we will discuss the following articles:
<ul>
    <li>Hepach, Maximilian, Ryan Bishop, J. R. Carpenter, Jussi Parikka, & Birgit Schneider. (2024). “Wind Humanities: An Elemental Media Approach.” Media and Environment, 6.2. https://doi.org/10.1525/001c.127444.</li>
    <li>Ingold, Tim. (2011). “Earth, Sky, Wind and Weather." Being Alive: Essays on Movement, Knowledge and Description. London: Routledge. 143-155.</li>
</ul>
If you are unable to access any of the selected articles through your home institution or local library, please contact us and we will work to secure you a copy.</p>
